EU sovereignty: Slab is sold as a cloud-rebellion alternative.
We do not load fonts from a third-party CDN at runtime. fonts.googleapis.com
and fonts.gstatic.com would expose the visitor's IP to Google on every page
load (cf. LG Munich case 3 O 17493/20). We refuse that exposure as a matter
of product policy.

To refresh, run: scripts/refresh-fonts.sh
